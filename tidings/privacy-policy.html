Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HJ App ("we", "us") handles information in Tidings (the "Game"), a cozy 3D trading game about carrying goods and news between small islands.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details. The Game does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.

Game data. Your save, language, sound and volume settings, and display preferences are stored only on your device, in the browser's local storage (or in the app's own storage if you use an installed version). We do not receive or store this data on our servers.

No advertising or analytics. The Game does not show ads and does not include analytics or tracking tools. It does not send your game data to us or to any third party.

2. Notifications

If you choose to turn on notifications in the Game's settings, your browser or device will ask for permission. When you allow it, the Game can show a notification when a voyage ends, a building is finished, or a contract is about to run out. These notifications are created on your device; nothing is sent to our servers.

3. Save Backups

The Game can export your save as a text code or file so that you can move it to another device or browser. The code contains only your game progress. Keep it private if you do not want others to see your progress. We never receive it unless you send it to us yourself.

4. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect information from the Game, we have no information to use. Game data is used only by the Game on your device, to save your progress and remember your settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Game data stays on your device. You can erase it at any time from the Game's settings, or by clearing the site's data in your browser or uninstalling the app. We take reasonable steps to protect the Game, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the Game does not collect personal information, there is nothing to delete, but if you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You control your data on your device: you can erase your save in the Game's settings, change or turn off notifications in your browser or device settings, and clear the Game's stored data at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about players, you can contact us with any question about this policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
