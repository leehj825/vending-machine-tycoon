⛵ Tidings

A cozy 3D trading game about carrying goods and news between small islands.

Sail between islands with a small boat full of goods. Every island has its own people, its own wares and its own worries. Buy cheap where things are made, sell where they are wanted, and carry news and orders from one shore to the next.

THE SEA

Eighteen low-poly islands, from tiny Isla Niebla to the big harbor of Port Verdant, and a great continent in the north. The sea ripples, the sun sets and night falls with the real time of day, and the seasons turn every few days, so one kind of good is worth more each season.

TRADE AND ORDERS

Every island sells local goods cheaply and pays well for the goods it wants. Prices drift over time. Today's News brings orders that refresh every ten minutes, and regular customers ask for big contracts you can deliver in as many trips as you like.

BUILD YOUR OWN ISLAND

Choose a home island and build on it: cottages, a storehouse, a market stall, a lighthouse and more. Your settlement grows from a camp to a town, and the residents you befriend can move in and bring their own small perks.

PEOPLE AND STORIES

Get to know the islanders and each one's story, which opens as your friendship grows. Three longer tales run through several characters, from a lost ship to a missing brother and a cake recipe. Rival captains sail the same seas, and every week there is a Trade Cup to win.

FEATURES

  • Three boats, from a balanced sloop to a big, slow barge
  • Crafting workshops that turn ingredients into finished goods
  • Festivals, storms, visiting traders and regattas
  • Daily goals and a collection book to fill
  • A small wind mini-game to speed up your voyages
  • Soft music that changes with the time of day, plus sound effects
  • Phone-first design, landscape support, and English and Korean
  • Accessibility options: text sizes, shapes instead of color cues, and calm motion
