🌙 Nightfall Settlement

Build a village, survive the nights, explore beyond the walls.

Get it on Google Play

Nightfall Settlement is a low-poly, quarter-view 3D action, survival and village-building game. By day you gather, build and grow your settlement; when night falls, enemies come for the walls and you fight to defend your people.

BUILD YOUR VILLAGE

Raise farms, camps, a blacksmith, a market and a well. Give your citizens jobs, watch their skills grow, and upgrade your worksites as resources allow. Wall in your settlement and post soldiers at the gates.

SURVIVE THE NIGHTS

Fight off nightly attacks with weapons you forge and swap on the fly. Dash, jump and use your class ultimate to turn the tide.

EXPLORE BEYOND THE WALLS

Lead expeditions with a squad of escorts, open chests, clear enemy sites and build camps that deliver resources to your village each morning.

FEATURES

  • Seasons, illness and travelling merchants shape each year
  • Touch controls with an on-screen joystick and pinch to zoom
  • Auto-save, with adjustable music, effects, text size and graphics
  • Play on Android
