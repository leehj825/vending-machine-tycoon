🌃 Nexus

A neon megacity. A dangerous secret. Three companions.

Android — coming soon

Nexus is a story-driven cyberpunk JRPG with flat-shaded low-poly 3D art and neon lighting. Ruen, Kyle and Yuna discover an ancient Data Core in the city's underbelly, and journey through restricted sectors to keep it out of the hands of the mega-corporations.

THE CREW

Ruen, an intuitive hacker, bends data streams with a floating orb. Kyle, a steadfast protector, can sacrifice his turn to block fatal blows aimed at his friends. Yuna, a fast independent ranger, delays enemies and breaks shields with twin plasma blasters.

EXPLORE AND FIGHT

Walk the alley and the Neon Market, a district that streams in as you explore, then head on to the Industrial Hub and the Corporate Spire. Husk patrols start real-time battles built on active-time-battle gauges and each crew member's own mechanic.

FEATURES

  • Story told through campfire and resonance scenes
  • Camp crafting, weapon upgrades, shops and side jobs
  • Generative synthwave soundtrack
  • Touch controls: on-screen stick, drag to turn the camera, pinch to zoom
  • English and Korean
