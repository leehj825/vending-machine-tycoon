⚔️ Vexmarch

Officers run your cities. Supply lines decide wars.

Get it on Google Play

Vexmarch is a real-time grand strategy game set in the ruins of the Vex Dominion. The emperor is dead, the throne is empty and six great houses claim the realm. Take one of them and fight for the crown.

RULE YOUR REALM

Thirty nodes make up the map. Manage your cities: set taxes, appoint governors, raise buildings and recruit soldiers. Officers run your cities, and their loyalty decides whether they serve you or leave you.

WAR AND SUPPLY LINES

Form armies and march them along the roads. Battles, sieges and morale matter, but supply lines decide wars: cut an enemy off and his army starves.

DIPLOMACY AND CAMPAIGN

Make alliances, break pacts and offer weak houses a merger. Play the story Campaign, a historical war of politics and betrayal, or a random Skirmish against 3 to 5 rival AI houses on Easy, Normal or Hard.

FEATURES

  • 3D strategy map with terrain, and a 3D city view with walls and marching armies
  • Walk mode: stroll your streets, talk to officers and townsfolk
  • Guided tutorial for your first game
  • English and Korean
  • Auto-save, with adjustable music, effects and text size
