2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Vending Machine City Tycoon is a casual single-player business-simulation game for ages 4 and up. The player starts with a little cash and one vending machine in a colorful 3D city, then grows a business: buying stock at changing warehouse prices, setting prices, restocking machines by hand or by truck, collecting cash, hiring staff, and unlocking new districts. It is entertainment: a relaxed idle/tycoon game that also works offline.

3. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES (no login needed)
- Launch the app and tap "Start game", then pick any empty slot. No account, sign-in, or sample files are needed.
- Follow the first-day guide at the top of the screen: tap a glowing ball over a shop front to buy a first machine.
- Tap "Walk" to walk the streets with the on-screen stick (or W A S D / arrow keys with a keyboard). Walk up to a machine and tap "Open" to see its shelves, then "Take cash" and "Refill".
- "Warehouse" buys stock, "Fleet" runs delivery trucks, "HQ" hires staff and opens districts, "City" shows the map.
- Optional: walking up to a vandal near a machine lets the player "Talk" them down or "Attack" (cartoon-style, no blood).
- "Menu" returns to the title screen, where saves can be loaded.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES AND TOOLS
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): shows a banner ad at the top of the screen and an optional rewarded video ad that the player chooses to watch for a bonus. The app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before personalized ads. The game plays normally if the player declines.
- Everything else (3D engine, graphics, sound, game rules) is bundled inside the app. There are no accounts, servers, payment processors, analytics services, or AI services. Game saves are stored locally on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. Only the ads that AdMob serves can vary by region and by the player's tracking choice.
