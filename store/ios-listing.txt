DESCRIPTION (max 4000) [2757]
Start your journey from a pocketful of change to a city-wide business empire!

In Vending Machine City Tycoon you don't just watch numbers go up. You run a real little business, and you do it on foot: walk the streets of a colorful 3D city, open your machines, and see the stock and the cash with your own eyes. Start with one humble machine on a quiet corner and work your way up to the richest tycoon in town.

BUILD YOUR EMPIRE
Put machines in the places where people are:
• Schools & Universities: catch the lunch rush with sandwiches and coffee.
• Offices & Shops: busy all day with snacks and gadgets.
• Gyms: protein bars for active customers.
• Hospitals & Subways: round-the-clock sales, day and night.
Buy permits to open new districts: Downtown, Eastside, Southside and Harbor, each with its own look.

WALK, OPEN, COLLECT
• Walk the city with the stick. Arrows point you to machines that are full of cash.
• Open a machine to see a close-up of its shelves and cash tray. Take the cash out, refill the stock, and watch it happen.
• Chat with passers-by and send them to your machines.
• Catch vandals kicking your machines. Talk them down, or throw a jab, a punch, a hook and a kick. Leave the kids alone!

LOGISTICS & STRATEGY
• Manage your inventory: keep the warehouse stocked with chips, soda, coffee, protein bars, sandwiches and tech gadgets.
• Dynamic market: warehouse prices move every day. Buy when they are low.
• Trucks and routes: send trucks to refill machines before they run empty.
• Set your prices carefully. Charge too much and customers walk away; too little and you leave money on the table.
• Upgrade machines with more capacity, cooling, ad displays and security. Watch the weather, rival companies and contracts.

HIRE HELP AS YOU GROW
Do it all by hand at first, then hand the jobs over: a purchasing agent buys stock within your limits, a truck driver runs the restock trips, a collector brings in the cash, a mechanic repairs machines, and a night guard chases off vandals. They all want paying every day, so choose wisely.

IDLE PROFITS & OFFLINE PLAY
Your business never sleeps. Even when you close the game, your machines keep selling for a while. Come back to a welcome-back report, collect the cash, and reinvest it. The game works without an internet connection.

FEATURES
• A bright, cartoon-style 3D city with many building and house designs, and people with their own hair and clothes
• A first-day guide that teaches you the ropes
• An end-of-day report with your sales and tips
• Reputation that boosts your sales as customers come to trust you
• Three save slots
• Music and sound with easy volume controls
