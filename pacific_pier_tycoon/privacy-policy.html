Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HJ App ("we", "us") handles information in Pacific Pier Tycoon (the "Game"), a 3D pier-building and management game for Android and the web.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details. The Game does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, and it has no analytics.

Game data. Your saved pier, settings (language, graphics quality, sound, accessibility options), album, progress, daily gift and the tutorial state are stored only on your device, in local browser storage. A local error log, which you can copy from the Game's menu if you want to report a problem, is also kept only on your device. We do not receive this data on our servers.

Advertising (Android app only). The Android app shows a banner and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your advertising ID and similar device data to serve and measure ads. Where required, such as in the EU and UK, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. The web version shows no ads.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the Game and save your progress and settings on your device
  • To show advertisements in the Android app, only if you choose to watch a rewarded ad

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Advertising in the Android app is provided by Google AdMob. Google's use of data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device's settings (Google > Ads).

4. Screenshots and Postcards

When you share a screenshot or postcard, the image is handed to the app you pick in your device's share sheet. If sharing is not available, it is saved to your device as a download. The Game itself does not upload these images.

5. Data Storage and Security

Game data stays on your device. You can erase your save from the Game's menu, clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the Android app to remove it. We take reasonable steps to protect the Game, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Permissions

The Android app asks only for internet access, which the ads need. Vibration is used for small feedback, and you can turn it off in the Game's settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

You control your data on your device: you can erase your save in the Game, manage ad personalization in your device settings, and clear the Game's stored data at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about players, questions about advertising data are best directed to Google.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
