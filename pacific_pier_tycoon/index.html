🎣 Pacific Pier Tycoon

Build, run and walk your own seaside pier. Fish, ride and rule the coast!

Free to play, with optional ads on Android

Start with a tiny wooden dock, welcome anglers and tourists, and grow it into a booming seaside resort, all while the tides, storms and seagulls try to ruin your day.

BUILD AND MANAGE

Lay decking and pilings, then place fishing shops, arcades, cafes, a Ferris wheel, a museum, a lighthouse and more. Anglers need quiet edges and bait, while tourists want rides, food and clean decks. Wood rots and storms hit hard, so repair planks, upgrade to iron and steel pilings, and build breakwaters.

WALK YOUR PIER

Switch to walk mode and explore your pier on foot. Fish by hand with a timing game, hunt legendary fish, dive for treasure, cook orders, play arcade or mini golf, pick up litter, help guests and unmask the mystery critic. Everything on foot is optional, so you decide how hands-on to be.

MAKE IT YOURS

Customize the deck, railings, lamps, shop names and your pier's name. Unlock outfits, titles and awards, host festivals and tournaments, and climb the regional ranking. Daily missions, a daily gift with a streak bonus, postcards and photo filters keep you coming back.

SEASONS, STORMS AND STORY

Seasons, tides, moon phases and weather bring red tides, rogue waves, pirates and rainbows. Story levels lead to an Endless Pier mode, and a weekly challenge brings a new twist every week.

FEATURES

  • Two kinds of guests with different wants
  • Upgrades, shop levels, staff, sponsor contracts, loans and insurance
  • Play in English or Korean, with text size, colour-safe and reduced-motion options
  • A left-handed layout and graphics quality settings for every device
  • Soft procedural music and sound
  • No accounts, and your progress is saved on your device
