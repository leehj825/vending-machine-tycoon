Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HJ App ("we", "us") handles information in Tiny Tides: Northern Voyage Tycoon (the "Game"), a cozy cruise-ship tycoon for Android and the web.

Summary

Tiny Tides has no accounts and no analytics, and the Game itself stores nothing on a server. Your save stays on your device. The Android app can show optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob). When you choose to watch one, Google may use the device advertising ID and similar device data to serve and measure ads, and in the EU and UK you are asked for consent first. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy. The web version shows no ads.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, settings (sound, graphics quality, accessibility options), postcard photos and daily-voyage results are stored only on your device, in local browser storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data deletes them.

Screenshots

If you choose to share a screenshot, it is handed to the app you pick in your device's share sheet. The Game itself does not upload anything.

Offline files

On the web, the browser keeps a copy of the game's files on your device (a service worker) so the game can open offline. These files contain no personal information.

Permissions

The Android app asks only for internet access, which the ads need, and the advertising ID permission that comes with them. Vibration is used for small feedback when you build or dock, and you can turn it off in the menu.

Children

The Game itself contains no personal-data collection or purchases. The only advertising is the optional rewarded video ads described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
